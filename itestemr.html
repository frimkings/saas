Migrating Eyes of Hope from iTestEMR

Eyes of Hope Vision Centre, Kasoa Branch · steps to bring their iTestEMR records into Nexus · prepared 21 November 2026

1Put the latest importer on the live server (once)

  • The basic iTestEMR importer is already on live (origin/main).
  • Not yet live: payments, balances owed, spectacle orders, drug prices, the capture tool's admin steps and the safer Undo. That code is still uncommitted on the local machine and needs to be committed, pushed and pulled on the server.
  • Make sure a worker is running for the imports queue. Imports run in the background: 
    php artisan queue:work --queue=imports

2Create the clinic on live

  • Create EYE OF HOPE on the Clinic Pro plan, with one branch named Kasoa Branch, time zone Africa/Accra.
  • Add the owner's login.
Add the six staff BEFORE importing. The importer matches each old visit to its clinician by name. Staff added afterwards leave every visit under the owner.
  • Clinician → Doctor
  • Administrator, Management → Manager
  • Head Optician → Optician
  • Customer Service → Secretary + Cashier

3Take a fresh copy on switch day

  • The 21 November copy misses anything recorded after it, so capture again on the morning of the switch.
  • Sign in to iTestEMR (hope.optocodesinnovations.org) with the administrator account. A clinician account cannot reach payments, balances, orders or prices.
  • Run the capture tool. It is on Platform → Legacy Imports → From other software: choose iTestEMR, then drag the button to the bookmarks bar, or copy the script into the browser console.
  • It takes about 5 minutes and downloads itestemr-hope-<date>.zip. Read the capture-report.txt inside it for any pages that failed.
  • Ask the clinic to stop entering data in iTestEMR once the capture starts.

4Import

  1. In Platform → Legacy Imports → From other software, choose the EYE OF HOPE clinic and the source iTestEMR (Optocodes).
  2. Upload the zip as it is; there is no need to unzip it.
  3. Click Check. Nothing is saved. Expect figures close to these, with no errors:
    ~930patients
    ~1,370visits
    ~1,060old payments
    8balances (GHS 1,755)
    ~283spectacle orders
  4. Click Import. The trial import took about 6 seconds.
The largest file in the zip (patient-records.html) is about 12 MB. The page accepts up to 20 MB, but the live server's PHP upload_max_filesize and post_max_size must be at least that large too.

5What comes in and how

  • Patients keep their KAS numbers. Next of kin is not brought in.
  • Visits bring complaints, vision, diagnoses, medications and refractions. Two visits on the same day become one.
  • Old payments are history on each patient's record, not new money in the books.
  • Balances owed become debts to collect, listed on Outstanding balances.
  • Spectacle orders are history only, added to the notes of that day's visit.
  • Products and services come in with their prices. Stock starts at 0.
  • Future review dates become pending appointments.

6After the import

  • Spot-check a few patients, the 8 balances on Outstanding balances, and a visit with a spectacle order in its notes.
  • Have each staff member sign in and change their temporary password.
  • Do a stock count on day one, so stock in Nexus starts correct. iTestEMR's own counts cannot be trusted.
  • If something looks wrong, Undo removes the whole import cleanly. Undo stops working once money has been collected on an imported balance.

7Local trial clean-up

  • The local trial database saas_eyeofhope is only a practice run and is not copied anywhere.
  • When finished with it, put the local .env back from .env.backup.
  • The backups stay in storage/app/client-backups/eyes-of-hope-itestemr, which git ignores. They hold patient data, so don't share them.

The 21 November backup, itestemr-hope-full-2026-11-21.zip, already passes the Check with no errors.
